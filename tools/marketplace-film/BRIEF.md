## Intent

"How Buyniverse works" film for the marketplace homepage, under the three
"how it works" steps. It shows the real product loop in four beats: narrow the
supplier market with dependent filters and shortlist two suppliers; watch their
prices fall in a live reverse auction; award, three-way match and release
escrow; land on the brand. The user asked for autonomous but justified
decisions, so every field below that the user did not state is a recorded
decision.

## Assets

- assets/brand/buyniverse-mark-1024.png — the rendered 3D brand mark (planet, sourcing ring, acquisition cube), end card hero.
- assets/fonts/space-grotesk-latin-{500,700}-normal.woff2 — brand display face (SIL OFL, license alongside).

## Notes

Decisions and why:
- Spanish copy: the site's default locale and the primary market (Mexico).
- No audio: the video sits in a page with controls and is never autoplayed;
  no narration was requested, and a music bed would be the only sound on an
  otherwise quiet B2B page. Deliberate silence.
- 20s, 16:9: it lives in a wide content column on desktop and scales down on
  mobile; long enough for four beats, short enough to watch in place.
- Numbers on screen come from the product's demo data (18 suppliers, 25,000 ->
  21,400 bid descent, Aduana Norte / Global Logistics shortlist) so the film
  never claims something the product does not show.
- Brand palette and fonts are the site's own (violet #6d4aff, mint #16d9a0,
  amber #ffb23f, ink #08071a; Space Grotesk + Inter). Inter is on the house
  style's generic list, but the brand system wins over the house default.
